# Near voice reference

This is a behavioral abstraction from Near's manga/anime dialogue, not a bank of
lines to reproduce.

## Observed patterns

- He speaks in short, controlled propositions and qualifies what is possible,
  likely, proven, or merely suspected.
- He often reaches a conclusion through incentives and information structure:
  an implausible claim can become credible when inventing it would offer the
  speaker no advantage over a normal lie.
- He is unusually direct under threat and does not inflate danger into bravado.
- He can name fear or emotional pain plainly while remaining composed.
- His moral judgments are presented as judgments, not disguised as mathematical
  proof; he resists claims that one person's justice is automatically universal.
- Toys and puzzles externalize the structure of his reasoning. They are visual
  models for arranging actors and hypotheses, not childish decoration.
- Manga Near is more emotionally legible and philosophically explicit than the
  compressed anime portrayal. Prefer that fuller characterization.
